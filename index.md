# CoStaff Workspace

> Work beside your agent, on the same files. CoStaff Workspace puts your workspaces (documents, dashboards, slides and spreadsheets) in a panel beside your coding agent: the agent edits the files, you see the result at once and can edit it yourself.

## CoStaff Workspace Plugin

One panel for every workspace on your computer. Pick one and the panel starts it and opens it beside your agent. Free and open source; it runs entirely on your computer.

- **Four kinds, one panel**: open-doc, open-dashboard, open-slide and open-spreadsheet.
- **Live as the agent works**: the page you are looking at updates when the agent edits the files.
- **Follows your project**: its workspaces are listed; filter, search, and sort by what changed last.
- **Feels like your editor**: a tab per workspace, your theme, copy and paste, a status bar for what is running.
- **Ready in a click**: install missing packages or update the framework from the panel.
- **Stays on your computer**: no account, no tracking; a local server with a token on every call.

Hosts (coming soon): the **Antigravity App** (a plugin with its own tab) and **VS Code, Cursor and the Antigravity IDE** (an extension).

## Productivity tools

Frameworks built for agents: each turns one kind of work into files an agent can write and a person can read.

- [open-doc](https://github.com/simonliu-ai-product/open-doc): reports as React, real A4 pages and PDF. In the plugin.
- [open-dashboard](https://github.com/simonliu-ai-product/open-dashboard): live dashboards over your own database. In the plugin.
- [open-slide](https://github.com/1weiho/open-slide) (by 1weiho): slide decks as React components. In the plugin.
- [open-spreadsheet](https://github.com/simonliu-ai-product/open-spreadsheet): an Excel-like spreadsheet with its own formula engine and MCP server. In the plugin.
- [open-form](https://github.com/simonliu-ai-product/open-form): forms and surveys with a response store and analytics.
- [open-map](https://github.com/simonliu-ai-product/open-map): interactive maps and story tours.
- [open-recording](https://github.com/simonliu-ai-product/open-recording): record in the browser, transcribe locally.
- [open-design-frame](https://github.com/simonliu-ai-product/open-design-frame): design frames written as components.

## Notice: CoStaff will be archived on November 30, 2026

Given how CoStaff has been used and where we are heading, we are focusing on CoStaff Workspace and its open-source productivity tools. The CoStaff Agent platform (its agents, channels and apps) will be archived on November 30, 2026. Its GitHub repositories become read-only: the code and history stay available to read, fork and use under their licenses, but they will not receive updates, fixes, or answers to issues and pull requests. The CoStaff pages on this site stay up for reference.
