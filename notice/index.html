Notice

CoStaff will be archived on November 30, 2026.

Published November 2026

Given how CoStaff has been used and where we are heading, we are putting our effort into CoStaff Workspace and its open-source productivity tools. The CoStaff Agent platform, with its manager, specialist agents, chat channels and business apps, will be archived on November 30, 2026.

What changes

  • Its repositories on GitHub become read-only.
  • They will not receive updates or fixes.
  • Issues and pull requests will not be answered.

What stays

  • The code and its history stay public, to read, fork and use under each repository’s license.
  • The CoStaff pages on this site stay up for reference.
  • The CoStaff Workspace CLI and this site carry on.

Thank you to everyone who used CoStaff, gave feedback, or contributed. What we learned building AI staff is what CoStaff Workspace is made of.
